# Computer Vision Assignment: "Image Processing Adventure Quest"
### Objective:

The objective of this assignment is to understand the fundamental concepts of image processing through a creative and interactive "adventure quest." You alone or your group will embark on a quest to explore different realms of image processing, solve challenges, and present their discoveries in a fun and engaging way.

### Instructions:

Let’s embark on an "Image Processing Adventure Quest." Each group will choose one of the realms below and complete ONE associated challenge. At the quest's end, each group will present their findings in a creative format such as a skit, comic strip, video, or storybook.

### Realms and Challenges:

**Realm of Pixels and Color Models**

**Challenge 1:** Create a visual poster that explains how pixels form digital images and how different color models (e.g., RGB, HSV) represent these images.

**Challenge 2:** Develop a short story or comic strip featuring a character who travels through the world of pixels and color models, encountering and learning from each element.
 

**Kingdom of Image Formats**

**Challenge 1:** Design a "royal banquet" menu where each dish represents a different image format (JPEG, PNG, BMP, TIFF). Describe the characteristics and uses of each format in a fun and appetizing way.

**Challenge 2:** Create a mock interview with characters representing each image format, discussing their strengths and weaknesses.
 

**Land of Image Enhancement**

**Challenge 1:** Write a short play or skit where characters use image enhancement techniques (e.g., histogram equalization, smoothing, sharpening) to solve a mystery or complete a quest.

**Challenge 2:** Create a series of before-and-after images that visually demonstrate the effects of different image enhancement techniques.
 

Empire of Image Segmentation
Challenge 1: Design a board game or card game where players use different image segmentation techniques (e.g., thresholding, edge detection, clustering) to achieve objectives or complete tasks.
Challenge 2: Create an illustrated guidebook that explains each segmentation technique and provides real-world examples of its application.
 

Dominion of OpenCV and Pillow
Challenge 1: Produce a fun video tutorial where your group explains how to use OpenCV and Pillow for basic and advanced image processing tasks, using props or animation.
Challenge 2: Create a "day in the life" diary entry from the perspective of a developer who uses OpenCV and Pillow in their daily work, highlighting key features and common use cases.
Deliverables:

A creative presentation (skit, comic strip, video, storybook, etc.) showcases your group's findings.
Visual aids or props used in your presentation.
A brief reflection (1 page MAX) from each group member on what they learned from the quest and how they approached each challenge. 
Evaluation Criteria:

Creativity and originality of the presentation.
Clarity and accuracy of the concepts explained.
Engagement and entertainment value.
Team collaboration and participation.
Overall presentation and use of visual aids.
